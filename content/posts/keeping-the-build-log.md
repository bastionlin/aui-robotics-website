Our project pages are a record of work in progress. Alongside a project's current status, we can share what changed, what we learned, and what comes next.

## What belongs in a build log?

- A short description of the problem and the approach.
- A tested result, with its limitations made clear.
- Photos or a video of the actual build, when available.
- The next question the team is working on.

## Share the useful parts

A useful update does not need to announce a finished robot. A mechanism that needs another iteration, a calibration note, or an explanation of a design decision can help the next person continue the work.
